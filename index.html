NanoOptic privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

NanoOptic is a camera app. It has no account, no ads and no servers of its own, and it does not sell or share your data. Your photos, videos and audio are saved on your phone and go nowhere unless you share them.

What NanoOptic reads and why

Portrait mode and Google ML Kit

Portrait mode uses Google's ML Kit library to find the person in the picture. The model is inside the app and the picture is processed on your phone. It is not sent to Google. ML Kit itself may send Google technical usage and performance data about the library (such as your phone model, Android version, the app's name and version, and timings) to improve it. Google describes this at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure. NanoOptic does not add anything to that data.

Network use

NanoOptic works without a connection. The only network feature is the optional web remote: a page you can open from another device on your own Wi-Fi to see the viewfinder and control the camera. It is off by default, needs a PIN that changes each time it starts, and is encrypted (HTTPS with a self-signed certificate) unless you turn encryption off. Nobody outside your network can reach it.

Crash information

If the app crashes, the details are saved on the phone only. They leave it only if you export the device report and send it yourself.

Signed captures

If you turn on "Sign captures", NanoOptic signs each file with a key that lives in your phone's secure hardware and writes a small record next to the file. The key never leaves the phone. The record lets someone with the file check that it has not changed since it was captured.

Children

NanoOptic is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

  • Switch off location, the web remote or signed captures in the app's settings at any time.
  • Deny or remove the camera, microphone or location permission in Android settings.
  • Delete your photos and videos like any other files. Uninstalling the app removes its settings.
